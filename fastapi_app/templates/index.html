About & how to use

What this application does

Assigns a new credit card customer to a behavioral segment (via KMeans clustering) and estimates their probability of churning (via a trained XGBoost classifier), based on account and transaction data.

How to use it

Fill in the customer's demographic, account, and last-quarter behavior details on the left, then click "Run assessment" to see their segment, churn risk, and the key factors behind that prediction.

What SHAP means

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) breaks down a single prediction into each feature's individual contribution, showing which factors pushed the churn estimate up or down and by how much.

Important note about predictions

This tool is trained on a public dataset for demonstration purposes. Predictions are estimates for a portfolio project, not a substitute for real risk assessment or financial advice.
